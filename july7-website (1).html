Under the red roof, since forever ago

JULY 7

A garden shed hangout tucked into Gokulpeth — corrugated roof overhead, greenery all around, and a pool table waiting inside. Nagpur's easy spot for long evenings.

See the Menu Get Directions

Part café,
part clubhouse.

July 7 sits inside Jagat Plaza at Law College Square, behind a wall of banana leaves and a roof painted the colour of an evening sky. Walk past the greenery and you're in — wood tables, warm lamps, and a pool table for the group that came to stay a while.

It's the kind of place people duck into for a coffee and end up staying for two hours, which, as it turns out, is exactly how long most people do stay.
